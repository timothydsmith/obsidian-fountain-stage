Fountain Stage is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) plugin for scripts written in [Fountain](https://fountain.io/syntax/), the plain-text format for scripts. It's made for plays: acts and scenes, entrances and exits, doubling, rehearsal pages, and PDFs in stage-play formats. It works on desktop and mobile, and it's in Obsidian's [community plugin directory](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/fountain-stage).

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Writing" icon="pencil">
    Formatting as you type, character names that complete themselves, Tab to change a line's type, page breaks as they'll print, and an outline with each scene's runtime.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rewriting" icon="document">
    Freeze drafts, keep what you cut, see what's changed since a draft, keep alternate lines, and work through rewrite passes scene by scene.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Checking" icon="approve-check">
    Names written two ways, stage directions an actor can't play, entrances and exits that don't add up, each character's voice, and the smallest cast that can play it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Printing" icon="open-book">
    PDFs in Modern Play Format, APT or Samuel French style, or a style of your own, in your own fonts, with locked pages and revised pages for rehearsal.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
